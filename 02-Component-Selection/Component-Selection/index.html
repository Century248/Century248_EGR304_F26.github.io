    Temperature Sensor

    1. DS18B20 * $6.93/each * DS18B20 | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. Digital output (easy MCU connection). | 1. Requires 1-Wire communication. | | 2. ±0.5°C accuracy. | 2. Slower temperature conversion. | | 3. Waterproof probe options available. | 3. Requires pull-up resistor. |

    2. ZTP-148SRC1 * $2.06/each * ZTP-148SRC1 | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. Non-contact temperature measurement. | 1. Requires additional signal processing. | | 2. Fast response time. | 2. More difficult to calibrate. | | 3. Includes ambient temperature compensation. | 3. Measures surface temperature, not air temperature. |

    3. LMT86LPM * $0.71/each * LMT86LPM | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. High accuracy (±0.4°C typical). | 1. Requires an analog MCU input. | | 2. Very low power consumption. | 2. Requires temperature conversion calculations. | | 3. Simple analog output. | 3. Sensitive to electrical noise. |

    2. NHD-C12832A1Z-FSW-FBW-3V3 * $10.95/each * NHD-C12832A1Z-FSW-FBW-3V3 | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. Low power consumption. | 1. Limited 128 × 32 resolution. | | 2. SPI communication. | 2.     3. MDOB128064V2V-WI * $12.30/each * MDOB128064V2V-WI | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. High contrast OLED display. | 1. Higher power consumption than memory LCD. | | 2. 128 × 64 resolution. | 2.     2. NJM7805FA * $1.85/each * NJM7805FA | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. Stable 5V output. | 1. Generates excess heat. | | 2. Up to 1.5A output current. | 2. Lower power efficiency. | | 3. Built-in short-circuit protection. | 3. Requires higher input voltage than output. |

    3. MIC5156-3.3YN * $3.84/each * MIC5156-3.3YN | Pros | Cons | |---|---| | 1. Provides 3.3V regulation. | 1. Requires an external MOSFET. | | 2. Low standby current. | 2.
